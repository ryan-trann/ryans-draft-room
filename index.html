How ranking works

Projected season totals for FGM, FTM, 3PM, OREB, DREB, AST, STL and BLK are standardized across imported players. Turnovers are reversed. When Ryan has drafted players, the fit score boosts categories where his projected roster is relatively weak. Roster eligibility is checked against PG, SG, G, SF, PF, F, C×2, UTIL×3, bench×3.

These are heuristic recommendations, not a full Monte Carlo simulation of other managers' future picks. No projection values are fabricated.
